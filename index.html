What are tag questions?

Tag questions are short questions added to the end of a statement. We use them to check information or to keep a conversation going. If the statement is positive, the tag is usually negative (You made a reservation, didn't you?). If the statement is negative, the tag is positive.

restaurant

Answer these questions out loud

1. What do you like about this business?

2. What don't you like?

3. What could they improve?

💡 This is self-practice — no microphone needed. Speak clearly and confidently.

Useful sentence starters

  • I really like the …
  • The thing I don't like is …
  • The service could be better because …
  • I think they should …
  • The biggest problem is …
  • On the whole it's good, but …
